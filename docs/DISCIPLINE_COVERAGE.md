# Discipline Coverage

The monitor uses multi-label categories: a spacecraft avionics role can appear in Aerospace and Hardware/Electrical, while a structures role can appear in Aerospace and Mechanical. This improves field-specific discovery without duplicating the underlying role or its notification identity.

## Maintained title vocabulary

- Mechanical: mechanical, electromechanical, mechatronics, thermal, fluids, heat transfer, product design/development engineering, design/test/validation, equipment, tooling, materials, structures, stress, and loads/dynamics.
- Aerospace: aerospace, aeronautical, avionics, propulsion, GNC, flight systems/sciences/controls/test/dynamics/mechanics, aerodynamics, aeroelasticity, aerostructures, airframe, aircraft systems/design/integration, spacecraft, space systems, mission systems/design/operations, airworthiness, payload, structures, stress, and loads/dynamics.
- Manufacturing/Industrial: manufacturing, industrial, production, process development, quality and supplier quality, tooling, materials and process, facilities, automation/controls, NPI, and continuous improvement.
- Product Management: product manager, technical product manager, associate product manager, product management, product mgmt, and APM. Product design, project management, and program management are not treated as PM by name alone.

The vocabulary is based on real employer naming. Boeing's early-career pages explicitly separate aerospace/avionics, mechanical/mechatronics, manufacturing, materials, industrial, and structural disciplines. RTX's early-career and operations programs use mechanical, aerospace, industrial, manufacturing, quality, mechatronics, aeronautical/astronautical, process-manufacturing, and production-support titles. Johnson & Johnson posts Product Management Intern and Product Management Co-op titles. These sources are useful regression references:

- [Boeing entry-level programs](https://jobs.boeing.com/entry-level)
- [Boeing engineering internships](https://jobs.boeing.com/internships)
- [RTX campus and early-career programs](https://careers.rtx.com/global/en/campus)
- [RTX Operations Leadership Development Program](https://careers.rtx.com/global/en/rtx-oldp)
- [Johnson & Johnson careers](https://www.careers.jnj.com/)

## Employer expansion

The company registry includes dedicated aerospace, automotive/EV, industrial automation, HVAC, semiconductor-equipment, medical-device, energy, engineering-services, and materials/manufacturing cohorts in addition to technology companies. The current registry has 407 employers; 75 declare mechanical-related role families, 34 declare aerospace-related role families, and 44 declare product-related role families.

Curated structured sources were added for Northrop Grumman, Varda Space, Johnson Controls, Medtronic, Nissan, and GE Vernova. The normal discovery layer continues probing official careers pages for the rest, and source success is reported separately from registry size.

## Featured designation and recommendations

The fire emoji means “featured major technology or engineering employer.” It is a curated discovery aid, not an objective ranking. `data/company_metadata.json` is the sole source for that designation.

Recommendation presets cover featured technology, Product Management, Aerospace, Mechanical, and Hardware/Electrical employers. They are starting points only: the notification UI selects nothing by default, and users can add or remove any company before opting in.
